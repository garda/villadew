01  ·  The story

Still mornings
over the rice fields

Villa Dew takes its name from the quietest hour of a Balinese day — just after sunrise, when the rice terraces still hold the night's dew and Canggu has not yet found its noise. That hour is the brand: soft light, still water, unhurried time.

Escape to Villa Dew, a two-storey Balinese sanctuary tucked among the rice terraces of Canggu. Four ensuite king bedrooms with soaring teak ceilings — one of them a garden cabin — sleep up to eight, wrapped around a private pool and tropical garden. Mornings begin with rice-field light and coffee on the terrace; evenings end a short stroll away at the warungs and restaurants of the neighbourhood, with Canggu's beach clubs and Seminyak minutes down the road. Newly renovated with a modern, elegant touch and cared for by a small team of friends, Villa Dew is the quiet side of Canggu — yours.

Where is Villa Dew?

Villa Dew is in the Canggu area of Bali, Indonesia — on a quiet private street in Kerobokan (Kuta Utara, Badung), with rice-field and mountain views. Canggu's beach clubs and Seminyak are minutes away by car, and Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) is about 14 km away.

How many guests does Villa Dew sleep?

Up to eight guests, in four ensuite king bedrooms: three in the main two-storey villa and one in a separate garden cabin.

Does Villa Dew have a private pool?

Yes. The whole villa is yours, including a private pool with a hammock, a tropical garden, a sun terrace and an outdoor shower.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Airport pick-up can be arranged on request.

What is included in a stay?

Air conditioning, fast Wi-Fi, smart TVs with streaming, a fully equipped kitchen, a teak dining table for eight, free parking on site and housekeeping.
